UseYourBrain

Problem Statement

Today, generative AI tools can give us quick answers to almost anything. However, if we start using AI for every question or decision, we may skip the process of thinking, researching, and coming up with ideas on our own.

UseYourBrain is a Java-based command-line project that encourages users to think about a problem themselves before depending on AI or other external help. It provides simple guided activities for making decisions, doing research, brainstorming ideas, and reflecting on the final outcome.

Scope of the Project

The project focuses on helping users:

- Think through decisions in a structured way
- Research a topic and compare different pieces of evidence
- Generate and evaluate their own ideas
- Reflect on their thinking and previous sessions

The application works through a command-line interface and stores completed sessions locally using a text file.

Target Users

UseYourBrain is mainly designed for:

- Students
- Learners
- People working on academic or personal decisions
- Anyone who wants to practice thinking independently before using AI tools

High-Level Features

- Decision Mode – Helps users think through a decision by considering different factors before reaching a final decision.
- Research Mode – Allows users to record what they researched, the sources they used, and evidence that supports or challenges their initial thoughts.
- Brainstorm Mode – Helps users generate ideas, consider their limitations, and choose a practical direction.
- Reflection & History – Allows users to look back at their current and previously saved sessions.
- Input Validation – Prevents common input errors such as empty responses and invalid menu choices.
- Persistent Storage – Saves completed sessions so they can still be viewed after restarting the application.
